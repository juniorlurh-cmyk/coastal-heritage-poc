# Coastal Heritage — first-call notes

## Suggested opening

"Good day, am I speaking with the person who looks after Coastal Heritage? My name is [Your Name]. I came across your business on Google while looking at construction companies in Pretoria. I noticed that you have a business listing and some positive ratings, but I could not find a current independent website that properly showcases your work. I build digital solutions, and I put together a website concept to show how Coastal Heritage could present its services, completed projects and customer feedback more professionally, while making it easier for people to request quotations. Would you be open to a quick look?"

## Value proposition

- A professional online home independent of directory platforms.
- A verified project portfolio with photos, locations, project scope and before/after examples.
- Clear service pages that help customers understand whether the company is the right fit.
- Customer reviews presented transparently, with direct links to original sources.
- Click-to-call and WhatsApp enquiry pathways.
- A lightweight analytics layer to measure visits, calls, WhatsApp clicks and completed enquiries after launch.

## Discovery questions

1. What is the correct trading name to use on the website?
2. Which services do you currently provide, and which are handled by qualified partners?
3. Which suburbs or areas do you serve?
4. What are the correct business address, contact number, WhatsApp number and operating hours?
5. Can you share 5–10 completed projects with photographs and short notes on the work done?
6. Which customers or projects may be named publicly, and do you have permission to publish their photographs or feedback?
7. Do you hold relevant company registrations, CIDB grading, B-BBEE certificate, NHBRC registration, insurance or trade certificates? Which are current and suitable to publish?
8. How do new enquiries arrive today, and who responds to them?
9. What type of work would you most like to win more often?
10. Would you prefer the first site to generate WhatsApp enquiries, calls, quote requests, or all three?

## Important approach

Do not say "you have no website". A directory references an old `business.site` address, but Google retired Business Profile websites in 2024. Say instead that you could not find a current independent website. Do not present stock photographs as their projects, do not claim the ratings are one combined score, and do not promise more leads or revenue before measuring a baseline.

## Suggested close

"The page you are looking at is a concept, not something I have published on your behalf. If the direction makes sense, the next step would be a short discussion about your actual services, project photos and the type of customers you want to reach. I can then tailor the content around the business and give you a clear scope and cost before anything goes live."
